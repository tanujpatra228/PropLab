    How PropLab works, and how far to trust it

    Lift and drag

    NeuralFoil, an AI model trained on thousands of XFOIL airfoil analyses, predicts how much lift and drag each blade slice makes, including stall and the extra drag small blades suffer. It runs right here in your browser.

    Thrust and power

    Blade Element Momentum Theory cuts each blade into 32 strips, finds the lift and drag on each, and balances that against the air pushed through the prop, standing still or moving forward.

    How accurate?

    Against wind-tunnel tests of six real APC props: within about 5% on thrust and 7.5% on power with the real blade shape, and about 11 to 14% with the simple classic shape.

    Strength and drone

    Spinning pulls each blade outward and thrust bends it; PropLab wants a 2× margin. It doesn't model flutter, so treat flexible blades with care. Hover time assumes calm air; real flights use more.
